## 2. Who this is for and what "great" looks like

The agent is a non-technical call handler, out of hours, often on a phone-sized screen, with a stressed customer (a pub manager, a hotel duty manager, or an engineer on site). They must never read out something false, never be asked to diagnose, never meet jargon, and always leave with a ticket number.

| Moment | Reassurance or friction | What the design does |
|---|---|---|
| Taking the customer's details | Friction (keep it quick) | One small card with three fields; it can come first or after the site; saved once for the whole call. |
| Deciding what is wrong | Friction (no judgement calls) | The agent only reads out a question and taps the customer's answer. The dashboard decides. |
| Reading words to the customer | Reassurance | Exactly one "🗣 Say to the customer" block per card. |
| Telling a customer it isn't ours | Reassurance (no dead end) | The Say names who to call and why, and the call is still saved, as a closed note. |
| Booking a kitchen one-off | Reassurance (high stakes: equipment switching later with nobody watching the dashboard) | A safety question, a check-before-booking card, "Booked" only once accepted, the exact times, Cancel on the site screen, and a ticket. |
| Finishing an issue | Reassurance | Check and log shows who called, what they said, what we found, what we did and what we'll log. One button. Then the ticket number. |
| Urgent P1 | Friction (speed matters) | One card, one red button. The words to read appear only once the P1 exists. |

## 3. The standard applied

### 3.1 Card anatomy (same as 144 §8.1, plus the question line)

| Part | Class | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | `.reco b.hd` | Plain words, the situation only. No emoji, no codes. |
| Say to the customer | `.say` (`data-testid="say-block"`) | Exactly one per card. Contractor flow: "🗣 Say to the contractor". Future tense ("I'll log it", "I'll make a note") on cards shown before logging. |
| Good to know | `.gtk` (`data-testid="gtk-block"`) | "ℹ Good to know (for you, not the customer)". Omitted when empty. |
| Small print | `.factline` (new, 12.5px secondary) | Kitchen live reading only, in plain words. |
| Question | `.stepq` | Starts "Ask the customer:" (or "Ask the contractor:") whenever the answer comes from the customer. Answer buttons are the customer's possible answers, in their words. |
| Buttons | `.outbtns` or `.chips` | Outcome cards: one primary "Check and log" (`data-testid="std-checklog"`). Question steps: the answers. |

### 3.4 When logging fails (P1, Check and log, closed notes, bookings)

Nothing that claims a ticket exists is shown unless the server returned a real ticket id. A reply without an id is a failure, never "#undefined".

| Path | What the agent sees on failure | Never shown on failure | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Red alert on the P1 card (card, details and notes kept): "⚠️ The P1 has NOT been raised: [plain message]. Nobody has been paged yet. Press Try again. If it fails again, phone the IoT helpdesk now on 023 814 0228." (`p1-raise-failed`) | "🚨 P1 raised", "ticket #N", `p1-dispatch-status`, the Say with "I've raised an urgent P1 ticket…", the "This call so far" entry | Try again re-sends the same P1 (button disabled while sending). A rare duplicate P1 after a lost reply is accepted: two pages are safer than none. |
| Check and log and closed notes | The 144 error box: "We couldn't log this: [plain message]" with Try again | "✅ Logged", "✅ Noted for the record", ticket number, "This call so far" entry | Try again, as pub heating today (no duplicate guard; R5). |
| `ctlRelease` | §7 | §7 | §7, with the server duplicate guard |
| Kitchen one-off booking | "We couldn't set that up: [plain message]. Nothing has been booked and nothing will be switched." (row K-book-fail) | "Booked", any time, any ticket number | "Log it for the IoT helpdesk" (row K-ncrit). If the booking is accepted but its ticket fails, the card says "Booked, but we couldn't log the ticket" with Try logging again, guarded by the booking id exactly as §7. |

The P1 Say block is rendered only inside the success branch, so the 153 sentence can never be read out after a failed raise.

The server never trusts times sent by the browser: at booking it reads the circuit's next times itself from the same live read and refuses the booking if they can't be read or differ from what the screen showed by more than 5 minutes. ### 8.3 How it works (server)

| Part | Design |
|---|---|
| Booking call | New `POST /api/oneoffs` `{ confirmToken, siteNo, deviceId, OohOneOffKind: 'extend' or 'earlier', OohOneOffMinutes: 30, 60 or 120, OohOneOffAttended: true, customerContact, callerWords, additionalNotes }`. Checks in order: one-off switch on; site confirmation consumed (C2, as every change); kill-switch; device found at the site, online, a Tuya switch with the `switch` command (`registry.js:71`), registered (`hasPublishedState`); live times read now; limits (below). | Limits | Up to 120 minutes. On later: start is the next off time, end is next off plus the minutes, and end must be before the next on time. On earlier: start is next on minus the minutes, must be in the future and after the next off time; there is no end step, because the timetable switches it on at its normal time anyway. Start within the next 24 hours. One booking per circuit at a time (a second is refused: "There's already a one-off booked for the fryer. Cancel it first."). |
| Where it is held | A document in the existing durable hold store `OohOverrides` (`services/overrides.js`), so it survives restarts and multiple copies of the server, with the existing lease so each step runs exactly once. ### 8.4 Safety notes

- It only switches power to the circuit; the appliance's own controls still decide whether it heats. The Say asks the customer to switch the appliance off at the appliance when they've finished.
- Nothing is booked unless the customer says someone will be in the kitchen.
- Limits of two hours either way, the next occasion only, within 24 hours.
- It never runs more than 10 minutes late; a missed step is reported, not retried later.
- A failed switch-off pages the on-duty manager after 10 minutes.
- It sits behind `OOH_KITCHEN_ONEOFF_ENABLED` (off by default) and the existing kill-switch. Before it is turned on, the build gate proves one booked on-later and one on-earlier one-off end to end on a fixture circuit, and one switch on and off on a live kitchen circuit with your go (the Tuya switch path in production must be proven first; earlier work found it could time out).

## 20. What does not change

No timetables, schedules or OptimumStartStop keys are ever written by this work; the kitchen one-off writes only the switch key the existing Turn on button writes, and a test enforces it. The control window's safety checks, three-outcome tracker and ticket model are unchanged (D2). Ticket types, tags, priority and the `[TRG]` anchors (`Operator:`, `Caller's words:`, `Outcome:`) are unchanged. Pub heating screens are unchanged apart from the "This call so far" fix; its tickets gain the customer contact line. The only new setting is `OOH_KITCHEN_ONEOFF_ENABLED` (off by default). Nothing is released without your go.
