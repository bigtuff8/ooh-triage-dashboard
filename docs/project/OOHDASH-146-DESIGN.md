## 2. Who this is for and what "great" looks like

The agent is a non-technical call handler, out of hours, often on a phone-sized screen, with a stressed customer (a pub manager, a hotel duty manager, or an engineer standing on site). They must never read out something false, never meet jargon, and always leave with a ticket number.

| Moment | Reassurance or friction | What the design does |
|---|---|---|
| Reading words to the customer | Reassurance | Exactly one "🗣 Say to the customer" block per card, visually distinct (tinted, coloured edge). Never two look-alike text panels. Contractor flow says "🗣 Say to the contractor". |
| Background the agent needs | Friction (keep it out of the script) | "ℹ Good to know (for you, not the customer)", shown only when it adds value. |
| Finishing an issue | Reassurance | Check and log shows what the customer said, what we found, what we did and what we will log, with the notes box. One button. Then "✅ Logged: ticket #N is with the IoT helpdesk". |
| Urgent P1 | Friction (speed matters) | One card, one red button "Raise the P1 and finish". The words to read appear the moment the P1 exists, so "I've raised…" is true. |
| A change sent to equipment | Reassurance | Only device-confirmed is "done". "I've logged it" only when a ticket number exists. |
| Routine "no further action" | Friction | Still one path through Check and log, still logged, never "End with no action" after something was done. |

## 3. The standard applied (shared structure)

### 3.1 Card anatomy (same as 144 §8.1)

| Part | Class | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | `.reco b.hd` | Plain words, the situation only. No emoji, no codes. |
| Say to the customer | `.say` (`data-testid="say-block"`) | Label "🗣 Say to the customer" (contractor flow: "🗣 Say to the contractor"). Exactly one per card. Future tense ("I'll log it") on cards shown before logging. |
| Good to know | `.gtk` (`data-testid="gtk-block"`) | Label "ℹ Good to know (for you, not the customer)". Omitted when empty. |
| Small print | `.factline` (new, 12.5px secondary) | Kitchen live reading only, in plain words ("Checked at 19:42: the fryer is off; its timetable has it on since 11:30."). |
| Buttons | `.outbtns` or `.chips` | Outcome cards: one primary "Check and log" (`data-testid="std-checklog"`). Step cards: the choices. |
