OOHDASH-123 Rosetta Stone - command outcome states

This is the operator-facing experience of sending a device command under Spencer's new ThingsBoard IoT Command Gate contract. Pick a device, then press the action and watch the honest three-outcome model play out: sent, then confirmed / not confirmed / not delivered. Tuya finishes in about half a second; Salus heating takes minutes, so the operator is released at once and told the result later. Nothing ever shows as success unless the device's own reading proves it. All device names and readings are real estate values.
