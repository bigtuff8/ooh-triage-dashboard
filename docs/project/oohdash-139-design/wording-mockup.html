OOHDASH-139: pub heating outcomes, wording for sign-off

What the operator sees when a caller rings about pub (Bar/Restaurant) heating at a site with a Lighthouse boiler panel (213 sites). The gate is held until you agree this wording.

Before you read: the live check changed three things

1. Valve alarms aren't live. On 6863 the zone-valve alarm flags are "true" on all zones, but they were last updated about 262 days ago. Meanwhile those zones are actually running. They are stale, so the outcomes below don't use valve alarms.

2. Panel alarms aren't being managed. 6863 has 6 open alarms (the oldest from Nov 2025) and 4741 has 8 (the oldest from Sep 2025). None of them are acknowledged, so they can't be used to say what is wrong tonight. The escalation email to Sam in the discovery doc says 6863 has a live zone-3 valve fault. The data doesn't support that, and the email needs redrafting before it goes.

3. The "warm-weather" flag doesn't stop the heating. We checked 178 panels over 21 days. The flag is on at most sites in cold weather, and the zones run regardless. Only the real summer-mode setting blocks heating, so the warm-weather outcome has been removed. The panel's actual on/off state always comes first.

4. Overrides can force heating OFF, not only on. This was proven across 27 V1 sites and several V2 sites. Some V1 zones have been forced off for 21+ days, so "forced off at the panel" is now its own outcome (W5). V2 overrides have a real end time. V1 overrides renew every 15 minutes, so for V1 we say "held" rather than giving a time.

Updated 2026-10-23 after a read-only sweep of the history on 135 V1 and 43 V2 panels. That answered four of the five open questions.
