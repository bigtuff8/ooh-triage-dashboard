even when a subsystem is unhealthy — because both K8s liveness AND readiness probes hit /healthz and,

pod (full outage + forced re-SSO). Body-level degrade makes state visible to monitoring/canary
without weaponising the probe. Within design §4.1's stated latitude; chose the safer option.
