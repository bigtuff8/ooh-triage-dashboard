P1 escalation outcome card — the two states

This is the exact card an OOH handler sees at the end of a system-decided P1 flow (outcomeP1, public/js/flows.js:166-174). The only thing that changes between the two states is the deployment value SMS_PROVIDER. Flip the switch below to see what today (log) looks like versus what enablement (twilio) delivers. No real phone numbers appear anywhere — the recipient is shown by a real name only (never the generic "On-duty escalation manager" default), exactly as the product must persist it. Mobile sign-off: this card is read on a phone out of hours, so all three states are laid out to reflow to phone width — the sent variant keeps the timestamp and the tappable ticket deep-link readable. Confirming that on a real phone-width viewport is a required live-test sign-off item (DESIGN-GATE.md §4).
